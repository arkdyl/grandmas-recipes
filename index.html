Pierogi

They take like 1.5 hour to make.

Ingredients

  • 500g flour
  • about 250ml of warm water
  • 2 spoons of oil
  • 600g of potatoes
  • 250g of white cheese [twaróg]
  • 2 onions
  • salt, pepper

How to make?

  1. Cook the potatoes and mash them
  2. Fry the onions
  3. Mix potatoes, cheese, onions, add salt and a lot of pepper
  4. Make dough from flour, water and oil
  5. Roll it out, cut circles with a glass, put filling inside and close them
  6. Cook in boiling water until they come up

psst.. grandma always says the dough has to be soft

Szarlotka [apple cake]

Ingredients

  • 400g flour
  • 200g butter [must be cold!!!]
  • 100g of powdered sugar
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • apples, around 1.5kg
  • cinnamon

How to make?

  1. Mix flour, butter, sugar, baking powder and yolks into a dough
  2. Cut it in half and put in the fridge for 30 minutes
  3. Grate the apples, add cinnamon and cook them a little
  4. Half of the dough goes on the bottom, then apples, then grate the rest of the dough on top
  5. Bake 180 degrees for 50 minutes

use sour apples, sweet ones don't work as good

Rosół [chicken soup]

Every Sunday at grandma's house :] [very delicous]

Ingredients

  • 1 chicken
  • 3 liters of water
  • 3 carrots
  • 1 parsley root
  • a piece of celery
  • 1 leek
  • 1 onion
  • allspice, bay leaf

How to make?

  1. Chicken in cold water, cook slowly, take the foam off the top
  2. Cook on low heat for 1.5h
  3. Add vegetables and cook 1 hour more
  4. Add salt. Eat with noodles

Naleśniki

Thin pancakes with sweet cheese.

Ingredients

  • 250g flour
  • 500ml milk
  • 2 eggs
  • salt
  • 400g of white cheese [twaróg]
  • sugar [grandma never measures it]
  • some sour cream

How to make?

  1. Mix flour, milk, eggs and salt
  2. Mix cheese with sugar and cream
  3. Fry them on a hot pan, they have to be thin
  4. Put cheese in and roll them
